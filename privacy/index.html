Privacy Policy

Home Energy Consumption is a private application used by its developer for personal home energy monitoring.

Information accessed

The application accesses information from the developer's own Google Nest devices through Google's Smart Device Management API. This may include thermostat status, temperature, humidity, operating mode, and HVAC activity.

Use of information

The information is used solely for personal home energy monitoring, analysis, and automation.

Storage and sharing

Data collected by the application is for the developer's personal use and is not sold or shared with third parties.

Access

The application is not offered to the public. Access to Google Nest data is authorized by the developer through their own Google account and can be revoked through their Google account settings.
